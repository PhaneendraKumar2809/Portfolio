Publication

Hand Gesture Recognition for Specially Impaired People Using IoT

International Journal for Research in Applied Science and Engineering Technology (IJRASET), Volume 14, Issue IV, April 2026, pages 3009 to 3016.

A wearable glove with five finger inputs and a tilt sensor that shows one of 192 messages on a display. The paper reports 96% recognition accuracy and a 78 ms average response time in prototype testing. I am the third author.
